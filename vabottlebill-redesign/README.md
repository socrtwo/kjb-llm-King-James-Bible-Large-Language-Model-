# Virginia Bottle Bill Organization - Website Redesign

A redesign of [vabottlebill.org](https://www.vabottlebill.org/), the website for the Virginia Bottle Bill Organization — a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working towards reducing litter, increasing recycling rates, and a sustainable future for Virginia by passing a Bottle Bill.

## About the Organization

The Virginia Bottle Bill Organization was started by three dog walkers that were fed up with litter they found on their daily walks. Their concern for the environment, frustration over Virginia's lousy recycling rates, and the production of ever-increasing mountains of single-use plastic and trash, made them band together to fight for change.

Passing a Bottle Bill in Virginia would greatly reduce beverage container litter, ensuring the recycling and reuse of containers while also reducing carbon emissions and the negative impact on the environment.

## What is a Bottle Bill?

A "Bottle Bill" is slang for "a beverage container deposit and redemption system." When you buy a beverage at a store, you pay the store a deposit on each bottle or can. When you return your empty can/bottle to the store or special redemption center, you get the deposit back. There are over 50 Bottle Bills in the world, including 10 states in the USA.

## Key Statistics

- Virginia currently recycles only **7%** of waste processed in permitted facilities
- Beverage containers make up **70%** of roadside litter in Virginia
- In states with bottle bills, beverage container litter has been reduced by an average of **80%**
- Virginia's PET bottle recycling rate is just **8%**, compared to Maine's 75% or Oregon's 71%
